01 — The grove

Two hundred trees.One family.

The estate sits on a south-facing slope where the picual olive has grown for four generations. We never blend, never buy in fruit. If the hillside has a bad year, so do we.

02 — The race

0hours

That's the longest an olive waits between branch and press. Oxidation starts the moment fruit is picked — so the mill sits in the middle of the grove, and harvest days end at the press, not the truck.

03 — The harvest

Eleven weeks,by hand.

04 — The press

Cold. Slow.Stubborn.

Granite wheels and patience, under 27°C, always. Yields drop by a third compared to heat extraction. Everything worth tasting stays in the bottle instead.
